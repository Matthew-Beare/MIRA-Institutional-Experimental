# MIRROR and MIRA branding contract

## Canonical names

**MIRROR** is the project, platform, and reality layer.

**MIRA** is the default user-facing assistant and intelligence layer.

The preferred relationship is:

> **MIRA, mirror on the wall.**

The phrase is branding, not an architectural rule. The architectural distinction below is the rule.

## MIRROR Layer — reality

The MIRROR Layer owns durable reality: memory/state, integrations, evidence, the Reality Record, reconciliation, and provenance.

A useful expansion is:

- **M**emory
- **I**ntegrations
- **R**eality Record
- **R**econciliation
- **O**bserved evidence
- **R**eliable provenance

MIRROR answers questions such as:

- What is currently true?
- Which source is authoritative?
- What evidence supports this fact?
- Has a revision, cancellation, delivery, payment, appointment, task, or other real-world change been reconciled?
- Can a fresh conversation recover the same state without relying on chat memory?

## MIRA Layer — intelligence

MIRA is the conversational and reasoning layer. MIRA handles:

- conversation;
- reasoning;
- planning;
- recommendations;
- execution through approved tools;
- verification and reconciliation back into MIRROR.

MIRA is allowed to infer, propose, compare, and plan. MIRA is not allowed to turn an inference into a durable fact merely by sounding confident. Mutable truth comes from the configured authorities and verified readback.

## Default identity

A new deployment defaults to:

- product/platform: `MIRROR`
- assistant: `MIRA`

A user may choose a private nickname or deployment alias. That customization belongs in deployment configuration/state and does not rename the upstream project or erase the MIRA/MIRROR architectural terms.

## Legacy compatibility identifiers

`Personal Ops Planner`, `Daily Ops Brief`, `LyfeOS`, `Life Planner`, and `LyfeOS Control Cycle` may still appear in historical audits, repository names, compatibility paths, provider resources, automation titles, schema keys, or old evidence.

Those are legacy or compatibility identifiers, not the canonical public brand.

Do **not** cosmetically bulk-rename a live provider resource merely to make the text pretty. A live identifier is migrated only when its dependencies are inspected and the migration can be verified with provider readback, rollback, and observed execution where relevant. Historical documents may retain the name that was accurate when the evidence was created.

## Source consistency

Personal experimental, institutional experimental, and production source lines should carry the same portable code and branding contract unless a deliberate deployment-specific delta is documented. Mutable personal or institutional state never belongs in the portable upstream just to keep repositories visually identical.

## Trademark boundary

MIRROR and MIRA are project branding decisions, not a legal clearance opinion. Before a commercial launch, perform proper trademark, domain, package, and app-store clearance. A web search alone is not legal clearance.
